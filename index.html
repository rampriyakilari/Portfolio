About

I teach computer science students how to build working systems and how to question them. My courses move from web applications to security, from machine learning to data visualisation, with hands-on practice in each.

After years in the classroom, I am now bringing that experience to corporate learning and development, where clear explanations and practical exercises matter just as much.

What I teach

Full stack web development

Students build and deploy complete web applications, from the interface to the server and database.

Cybersecurity

Learners study common threats and practise defending systems against them.

Cryptography and network security

Covers how encryption, keys and secure protocols protect data on a network.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning

Students train models, test them, and learn to question what the results really mean.

Data visualisation with Tableau

Turning raw data into dashboards that people can read and act on.

Robotic process automation with UiPath

Automating repetitive business tasks with software robots, step by step.
